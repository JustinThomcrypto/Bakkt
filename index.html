Moving clients from agreement to activation

A signed agreement is only the beginning. Before going live, institutional partners typically move through onboarding, compliance review, technical integration, treasury setup, and production approval. This simulated hub shows how a Client Success Manager can help coordinate those workstreams, identify potential delays early, and move trading and payments partners from agreement through activation to measurable results, including user adoption and transaction volume.

Quarterly Client Review: Halcyon Digital Markets

An example of how I would review goals, performance, delivered value, unresolved issues, and next-quarter priorities with a live client.

Goals agreed at onboarding

  • Replace two bilateral liquidity relationships with a single API integration by end of Q1.
  • Reach 20 million dollars of monthly notional across spot and OTC within two quarters.
  • Cut settlement breaks on crypto legs to under one per hundred tickets.
  • Keep U.S. dollar prefunding under a defined internal ceiling.

What launched this quarter

  • Trading infrastructure in production across two desks, 62 named users.
  • Digital asset OTC enabled for block tickets above a set size.
  • Stablecoin settlement enabled for end-of-day netting, in pilot with the treasury team.
  • Two operations staff trained on the break and exception workflow.

What we solved, and what is still open

  • Solved. Credential rotation failures. Root caused with engineering, client runbook updated, zero recurrences in six weeks.
  • Solved. End-of-day reporting mismatch against the client's internal ledger, corrected in the file spec.
  • Open. OTC block flow is still routed bilaterally. Client is waiting on an internal risk sign-off, not on us. Owner is the client's CRO, revisit in November.
  • Open. Two support cases above the agreed response window in August, both during a market-wide volatility event. Staffing pattern raised internally.

Renewal: March 31, 2027

  • Opened at the December review rather than in February, so neither side negotiates against a deadline.
  • To be true by December: stablecoin settlement out of pilot, volume within ten percent of plan or the plan reset, and no repeat of the August response time misses.

Next quarter plan

  • Move stablecoin settlement from pilot to production for all end-of-day netting. Target mid Q4.
  • Second training session for the newly hired operations analyst.
  • Support the client's internal risk paper on moving the OTC block flow, without owning its timeline.
  • Agree the volume plan for next year at the December review, alongside the renewal.

Where the relationship could grow

  • The client settles supplier invoices into two Asian markets through its bank. Cross-border payments could be a natural second product, subject to confirming corridor coverage, client eligibility, and applicable approvals.
  • Their retail affiliate has asked about an embedded buy flow. That is a Bakkt Widget conversation, and it belongs to sales, so it gets flagged rather than pitched here.
  • Neither is proposed at this review. Both are noted, with the introduction offered only once the stablecoin settlement rollout is complete.
